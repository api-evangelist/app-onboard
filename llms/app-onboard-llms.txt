# AppOnBoard, Inc. (Quvy)

> AppOnBoard, Inc. is a Los Angeles-based technology company founded in 2016 that builds
> AI-driven creative and advertising tools for app and game marketers. Its best-known
> product is Buildbox, a no-code platform for making games and apps without programming,
> and its current flagship is Quvy, an AI ad-creation and testing platform that generates
> static, video, and concept ad variations and predicts their performance against
> synthetic audiences before any real ad spend. AppOnBoard is backed by 500 Global; the
> original apponboard.com domain now serves the Quvy product site at quvy.com.

## Important for agents

- **AppOnBoard publishes no public API.** There is no developer portal, no API reference,
  no OpenAPI or AsyncAPI document, no SDK on any package registry, no CLI, no webhook
  catalog, no MCP server, and no A2A agent card. Verified 2026-08-12 by anonymous probe
  of every AppOnBoard / Quvy host.
- **Do not attempt to call an AppOnBoard API.** The only machine-readable surface is the
  Quvy web application's own AWS AppSync GraphQL backend. It is undocumented, is not
  offered as a product, and rejects anonymous requests with HTTP 401. It is not a
  supported integration point and must not be treated as one.
- **Quvy is used through its web application**, not programmatically. Sign in at
  https://www.quvy.com/login/ and run simulations in the product UI.
- `apponboard.com` answers HTTP 200 with the Quvy marketing homepage for **every** path,
  including paths that do not exist. Do not read a 200 from that host as evidence that a
  document exists.

## Product

- [Quvy](https://www.quvy.com): AI ad creation and simulation — generate static, video and
  concept ad variants and predict performance against synthetic audiences before spending.
- [Buildbox](https://www.buildbox.com): no-code game and app creation platform.

## Pricing

- [Quvy pricing](https://www.quvy.com/pricing/): tiered subscription metered in
  simulations and credits. Individual tiers (Starter, Premium, Pro) and business tiers
  (Starter, Professional, Advanced, Enterprise) are named on the page, but prices render
  client-side and are not readable anonymously. Billing runs on Chargebee and Stripe.
  A free entry point is offered ("Start Free"); no free trial of paid tiers is offered.
